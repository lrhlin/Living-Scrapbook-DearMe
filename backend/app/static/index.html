BEHIND THE LITTLE MAGIC

Little moments.
Pages that stay.

Collect your words and stickers. Dear Me brings them together.

Put a story on the page.

Choose stickers in My scrapbook, add your words and click Add. Dear Me arranges them into pages and saves the entry in your collection.

Bring a real moment.

In Sticker studio, upload a photo, pick a subject and choose an illustration style. A pet, a place or a favorite object makes a lovely starting point.

Bob illustrates your subject.

IBM Bob turns the subject in your photo into an original vector illustration, keeping its distinctive colors, shapes and details.

A finishing touch.

The app adds a rounded white border that follows the illustration’s shapes, then creates an SVG and a transparent PNG for you.

Keep it, use it, make more.

Your stickers stay in the library, ready for another story. Select Use in scrapbook to add one to your next entry, or download it to use elsewhere.

Make it a collection.

Come back to your saved entries, turn through their pages and read the words you kept. Download a single page as SVG or PNG, or gather the whole entry in a ZIP.
